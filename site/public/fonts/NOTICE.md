# Fonts

The landing page ships three typefaces, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The copyright notices are embedded in the font files.

- Inter (`inter.woff2`)
- DM Sans (`dm-sans.woff2`)
- Fragment Mono (`fragment-mono.woff2`)

They come from the Fontsource packages `@fontsource-variable/inter`, `@fontsource-variable/dm-sans`
and `@fontsource/fragment-mono`.
